Privacy Policy

EasyKazakh is a Kazakh language lesson app made by Madi Yedenbay. It teaches similar sounds and grammar. The lessons are stored in the app and work without an internet connection. The app does not ask anyone to create an account.

Information collection

EasyKazakh does not collect personal information, does not show ads, and does not send usage logs, device identifiers, or location to the developer.

Children

The app can be used by children who are learning Kazakh. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app does not include analytics, advertising, or other third-party services that collect data. The stores that distribute the app (Google Play, the App Store) have their own privacy policies.

Changes

This policy is effective as of 2026-11-25. Updates will be posted in this file.
